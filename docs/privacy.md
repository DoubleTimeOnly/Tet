# Tet privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Tet is a personal app. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no
analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Tet (flashcards, notes, tasks, habits, review history,
settings) is stored locally on your device. The developer cannot see it.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google" in Settings, Tet asks for the Google Drive
`drive.file` permission. This lets Tet create and manage **only the files it
creates itself** in your Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file.

- Tet uploads backup snapshots of your Tet data to a "Tet backups" folder in
  your own Drive, and deletes its own older snapshots to keep the most recent
  ones.
- Your Google account's email address is shown inside the app so you can see
  which account is connected. It is not stored or sent anywhere else.
- Backups go directly from your device to Google. They do not pass through any
  server operated by the developer.
- You can stop at any time with "Sign out" in Settings, and revoke access in
  your Google Account under Security → Third-party access. Backups already in
  your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Tet's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other services you choose to connect

If you add a Readwise token or a YouTube API key in Settings, Tet sends
requests to those services from your device to fetch the content you asked for.
Those keys are kept in the device's secure storage and are not included in
backups.

## Sharing

The developer does not collect, sell or share your data with anyone.
